# CircuitMarket

**A Nigeria-first electronics marketplace and price intelligence
platform.**

CircuitMarket is being built to make electronics sourcing and price
discovery more transparent, accessible, and dependable, starting in
Nigeria.

## What CircuitMarket does

CircuitMarket brings together two connected systems built around a
shared product catalogue.

### 1. Marketplace

A place for buyers to discover electronics, components, spare parts,
repair tools, and accessories from sellers.

The marketplace is designed for: - Buyers and electronics enthusiasts -
Phone and electronics technicians - Retailers and wholesalers -
Spare-parts dealers and suppliers

Sellers will be able to manage their stores and listings, while buyers
can explore products and request seller contact through controlled
contact-sharing workflows.

### 2. PriceCheck

A price intelligence system that collects and reviews real-world price
observations.

PriceCheck is designed to organize price information by: - Exact product
and variant - Condition - Amount and currency - Country and location -
Observation date - Evidence and review status

**Seller asking prices are kept separate from reviewed price
observations.** Marketplace offers are not automatically treated as
verified market prices. Only qualifying, reviewed observations
contribute to published PriceCheck ranges.

## Product coverage

The catalogue is designed to support: - Finished electronics - Spare
parts and components - Repair tools and equipment - Accessories -
Related electronics products

Condition support includes new, used, and refurbished products, with
parts-specific distinctions such as new aftermarket and used or salvaged
components.

## Geographic scope

The initial focus is Nigeria, with a nationwide ambition. The platform's
geographic structure is designed to support country, state, local
government area, city, and area-level information where sufficient
reliable data exists.

The architecture is intended to support expansion into other countries
over time.

## Trust and data principles

CircuitMarket is being developed around several principles:

-   Keep seller offers distinct from verified price intelligence.
-   Preserve product and variant accuracy.
-   Use evidence-backed price observations and review workflows.
-   Apply moderation and seller-accountability controls.
-   Protect private seller contact information.
-   Make useful market information easier to contribute to and
    understand.

## Development status

CircuitMarket is under active development. Its backend foundation
includes structured product and geographic catalogues, seller profiles,
marketplace listings, price observations, review and moderation
workflows, and price-range calculation infrastructure.

The first release will be developed incrementally, with a focus on a
reliable core experience before expanding into additional features and
regions.

## Mission

**Make electronics sourcing and price discovery more transparent,
